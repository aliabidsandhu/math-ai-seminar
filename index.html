About the series

The Mathematics and AI Seminar Series brings together mathematicians, researchers, educators, and students to discuss the growing connections between mathematics and artificial intelligence. Talks will explore AI in mathematical research and teaching, including its potential, limitations, and challenges. Our goal is to encourage thoughtful discussion and collaboration across institutions.
